# Microwave Backhaul QC

- [ ] Model, frequency assignment, license/authorization, configuration and endpoint identity verified
- [ ] IDU/ODU/RFU/antenna inventory and alarms recorded
- [ ] Power, grounding and surge protection records reviewed
- [ ] Management/NMS access and time synchronization checked
- [ ] Both-end RSL, modulation, ACM, ATPC, XPIC/protection (if supported) recorded
- [ ] Ethernet/IP handoff, VLAN, MTU, QoS and counters tested
- [ ] Capacity, latency, loss and availability evidence checked against approved design
- [ ] Alarm/event log and configuration backup captured
- [ ] Field installation and tower safety sign-off handled by qualified personnel
