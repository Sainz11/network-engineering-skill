# Outdoor Radios and Wireless Bridges

## Families
- Cambium ePMP Force/AP, PMP 450, PTP, cnWave
- MikroTik QRT 5/QRT 5 ac, LHG, SXTsq, NetMetal, mANTBox
- Ubiquiti NanoStation, NanoBeam, PowerBeam, LiteBeam, Rocket, airFiber
- TP-Link Pharos CPE/WBS
- Ruijie/Reyee RG-EST wireless bridge

## Health indicators
Registration/association; RSSI/RSL per chain; SNR; noise floor; MCS/modulation; channel utilization; retries; CCQ where applicable; Ethernet negotiation/errors; latency/loss; throughput; uptime; temperature/alarms.

## Design
PTP vs PTMP; bridge vs routed; frequency reuse; channel width; polarization; antenna gain; Fresnel clearance; fade margin; regulatory power; VLAN transparency; QoS; synchronization.

Do not align or install equipment at height without qualified personnel and approved site safety procedures.
