# Access Points and WLAN

## Vendors/families
Ruijie/Reyee RG-RAP; Huawei AP including AP661; Ubiquiti UniFi; TP-Link Omada; MikroTik wireless; Cisco WLAN; ZTE and other operator APs.

## Validation
- Correct power/PoE class and injector/switch budget
- Controller/cloud adoption and management reachability
- SSID/security/authentication
- VLAN mapping and DHCP reachability
- Channel, width, power, band, DFS/regulatory settings
- Client count, retries, airtime, RSSI/SNR, roaming
- Firmware compatibility and config backup

## Troubleshooting
Separate wired uplink issue from RF issue. Verify switch port/VLAN/DHCP first, then AP state and client association, then RF/channel/interference and roaming.
