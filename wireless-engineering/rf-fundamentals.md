# RF Fundamentals

Key terms: frequency, wavelength, channel width, EIRP, antenna gain, polarization, RSSI/RSL, SNR, noise floor, MCS, modulation/coding, retries, channel utilization, fade margin, path loss, Fresnel zone, DFS/TPC.

Interpret metrics together. Strong RSSI with poor SNR can indicate interference/noise. Unequal chain levels may indicate polarization, cable, connector, or antenna issues. High retries and low throughput can occur despite association.

Never increase transmit power beyond regulatory or vendor limits to mask a poor link.
