# PTP and PTMP

## PTP
Two endpoints form a dedicated link. Validate both ends, path profile, alignment evidence, channel coordination, capacity, latency, redundancy, and VLAN/L2/L3 handoff.

## PTMP
An AP/base station serves multiple subscriber modules. Validate sector capacity, subscriber distribution, airtime/scheduler utilization, interference, synchronization, QoS, and per-subscriber service levels.

## Acceptance
Record baseline and post-test RSSI/RSL, SNR, modulation, retries, channel utilization, Ethernet counters, throughput, latency, packet loss, and alarms. Test in an approved window or isolated lab.
