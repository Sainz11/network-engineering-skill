# Radio, Site, and Physical Safety

- Confirm country/region, allocated band, licensing status, channel restrictions, EIRP limits, DFS/TPC rules, and site permissions.
- Use current vendor planning tools and regulatory profiles.
- Record coordinates, antenna height, antenna model/gain, cable/connector losses, polarization, frequency, bandwidth, transmit power, and path profile.
- Assess LoS, Fresnel clearance, terrain, buildings, vegetation, reflections, interference, fade margin, and availability.
- Tower/roof work, lifting, rigging, electrical work, lightning protection, and antenna installation are restricted to qualified, authorized personnel following employer procedures and applicable safety rules.
- Do not climb towers or access rooftops for tests without formal authorization, training, supervision, PPE, rescue plan, and site-specific risk assessment.
- Never bypass interlocks, grounding, surge protection, or regulatory power limits.
